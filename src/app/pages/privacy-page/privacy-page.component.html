Privacy and data handling

QueryLens runs locally in your browser. Your datasets stay on your device and are never sent to QueryLens servers.

What is stored locally

Imported files, fetched API responses, saved snapshots and your theme preference, in this browser profile only. Clearing your site data, or using Settings, removes them.

What is not collected

No account identifiers, no uploads of your records, no non-essential cookies and no tracking scripts.

Requests to APIs you choose

When you fetch a dataset from an API, the request goes straight from your browser to that address, with the token and headers you provide. QueryLens never sees them.

Your responsibility

You must be authorized to process the data you load, and to follow your organization's data policies.
